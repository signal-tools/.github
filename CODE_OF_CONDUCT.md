# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in Signal Tools to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Examples include:

- giving and accepting specific, technically grounded feedback;
- respecting different experience levels, backgrounds, and viewpoints;
- focusing disagreement on ideas and observable behavior; and
- taking responsibility for mistakes and repairing their impact.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, personal attacks, and
publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in project repositories, issue trackers, review threads, and other spaces where someone
represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the organization owner through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/jonathantneal). Include relevant links and context. Reports will be reviewed
promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may
temporarily or permanently limit participation when behavior conflicts with this standard.
